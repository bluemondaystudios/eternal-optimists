About our school

Knowledge gives life.

Eternal Optimist Combined Pre-Primary School is an English medium private school in Glen Cowie, Limpopo. We welcome children from 2 years old right through to Grade 2, so your child can grow with us through their most important early years.

Our days are full of learning, play, music and celebration — from Career Day and Baking Day to Heritage Day and our end-of-year concert. We believe every small step today builds towards big dreams tomorrow.
